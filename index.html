01 / BSCS 1C

About Us

Welcome to 1st Year Bloc C! We created this site so you can get to know each student in our class. Not only do we talk to each other inside the classroom, but we also hang out after school, review together for upcoming quizzes, and help teach one another. There is never a dull moment with us — even during class, it's always full of laughter. Here, you'll discover our true selves, our hobbies, and everything else you might want to know about our bloc. We invite you to explore, learn, and grow with us, one post at a time. Thank you, and we hope you have fun scrolling!

Talde, John Dave D.

For my CS101 final project, I made my About Me page better and gave it a space futuristic theme, using only HTML and CSS. It has all the required sections like Hello, About me, My path into computing, Favorites or skills, Goals and Reflection, plus my class schedule in a table and an Ask me about part where you can click a question to see my answer. I also added my favorite song, Backburner by NIKI, and small icons like a basketball and a game console that spin when you hover over them. I used the Pixelify Sans and Tektur fonts so the page looks like a game, and I made sure it fits on a phone screen too. The hardest part was fixing my file paths and the layout on mobile, but now I think the page really shows who I am as a first-year BSCS student.
