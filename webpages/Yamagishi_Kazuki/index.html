A SNIPPET ABOUT ME

I'm someone who thinks a lot, plans occasionally, and procrastinates more than I'd like to admit. I have ambitions, although my actions don't always reflect them. For now, I'm still figuring things out, one deadline at a time.

"Comfort kills ambition." —unknown author

I believe in this phrase, but ironically, I don't really live by it. I know that staying in my comfort zone can hold me back from becoming the person I want to be. Yet, I still find myself doing the same things over and over again. Maybe I know what I need to change, but I'm still struggling to actually do it.

THINGS IM TRING TO WORK ON:
  • Being more consistent instead of relying on motivation
  • Taking responsibility before things become urgent
  • Learning to manage my time
  • Turning plans into actual progress
FEW THINGS ABOUT ME:
  • Currently studying Computer Science
  • Interested in how things work
  • Has a complicated relationship with deadlines
  • Likes to learn new things
  • Easily gets bored

I may not have everything figured out yet. Maybe that's the point. I'm not here to pretend I've become the person I want to be. I'm here, still figuring out how to become him.
