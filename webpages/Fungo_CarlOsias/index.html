My Path Into Computing

Growing up in the province without access to computers, my early years were defined by competitive math rather than technology. Entering college applications without a clear direction, my ability to function under pressure kicked in, and I chose Computer Science because it offered the most math-skewed path among tech fields. Although financial constraints kept me from studying math at UP, choosing BU became the most practical way forward. It was a choice born of circumstance, but one I am genuinely proud to step into today—ready to embrace the journey, learn deeply, and make my time in CS truly meaningful.

My Reflection

Honestly, this felt way more like a personal reflection than a coding project. If I'm gonna say what i think, the hardest part wasn't even writing the HTML and CSS, but figuring out how to actually show who I am without it feeling forced. Having a starting structure made getting off the ground a lot easier.

I struggled a ton getting CSS to work right with the HTML at first. Between digging through resources and just pushing through the errors, it was a headache, but I managed to pull it off.

Balancing actual code with a personal intro was trickier than I expected. Fixing all those layout and styles showed me how much tiny CSS details change the whole look. In the end, building this retro windows style page taught me web basics and gave me a better feel for how to present myself as a CS student.
