06 / WHAT I LEARNED

Reflection

Making this webpage helped me understand that creating a website involves more than writing text and adding pictures. I also need to think about the layout, colors, spacing, readability, and how visitors will move from one section to another. I learned that HTML gives the page its structure, while CSS controls how the page looks.

Compared with my previous About Me activity, I improved the organization of my content and added more sections to share information about myself. I also worked on the navigation, card layout, table design, and page appearance. Using Flexbox helped me arrange content more neatly, while hover effects made the links and cards feel more interactive.

I still have more to learn, especially when it comes to making a design work well on different screen sizes. This project reminded me that I can improve my work by testing it, finding mistakes, and making changes. I am proud that I was able to turn my simple About Me page into a more complete webpage.
