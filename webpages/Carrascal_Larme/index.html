A curious mind with a creative spirit.

I'm Larme, a student exploring the meeting point of art and technology. I enjoy creative work, especially 3D modeling, visualization, animation, and experimenting with digital tools. where ideas can become objects, scenes, and entire environments.

Outside the creative process, I am curious about how computers work and how programming can solve real problems. I like learning by experimenting, making mistakes, and finding ways to improve. For me, creativity is not limited to making something beautiful; it is also about making something useful.

I am still discovering what I can do, and that is what makes the journey exciting. Every new skill is another instrument added to my own symphony.

A reflection in progress

Creating this page helped me understand that web design is more than placing text and images on a screen. It is about organizing information, choosing a visual direction, and making sure that every element has a purpose. I learned how HTML gives a page its structure while CSS shapes its appearance and movement.

Compared with a simple About Me page, this version gives my interests and goals a clearer identity. I explored layout, spacing, color, typography, and CSS animation to create a more complete experience. I also learned to consider mobile users and accessibility, including readable text and meaningful image descriptions.

This project reminded me that a polished result comes from many small decisions. I still have much to learn, but I am proud to keep experimenting and improving one step at a time.
