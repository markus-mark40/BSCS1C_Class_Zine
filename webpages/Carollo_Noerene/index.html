get to know, Noerene

Welcome to my corner, where each piece of me adds to the picture.

Hello there! I am Noerene Carollo, a 1st year BSCS student at Bicol University. Outside academics, I find peace in sleeping especially during a rainy day. When awake, you can find me leisurely wasting time on the internet watching videos, reading manga/manhwa/manhua, or playing online games. I also like to repeatedly listen to the songs over and over again until I get tired of them.

My Path into Computing

My curiosity and drive to know how various things work was what pushed me to pursue Computer Science. I like knowing what happens behind a screen: how an app opens, how a game connects players, how a page is built. Aside from that, due to my indecisiveness and the struggle of finding what I am really passionate about back then, I decided to chose practicality given the circumstances. Which is what brought me to create this webpage.

Reflection

Making this page taught me that web design is mostly about small decisions that add up: a color palette, two fonts, and consistent spacing can change how a page feels. I learned how semantic tags give a page structure, how Flexbox and Grid arrange content, and how CSS variables let me change a whole theme from one place.

Compared with my About Me activity, I added new sections, a color palette, a skyline background, a bookshelf, a spinning vinyl, hover effects, a dark mode, and a layout that works on a phone.
