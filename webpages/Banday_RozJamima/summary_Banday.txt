## PROJECT OVERVIEW

This website is my personal "About Me" website for our CS101 project. It contains information about me, my hobbies, interests, favorite things, and goals. It also shares a little about my experience as a first-year BSCS student and what I have learned about computing.

## DESIGN CONCEPT

The design of my website is inspired by Disney's *Tangled* and Rapunzel. I chose this theme because I like its dreamy and fairytale style.

The design includes:

- Lavender, pink, purple, and gold colors.
- A soft and magical fairytale background.
- Golden sun designs inspired by Rapunzel.
- Butterflies, flowers, moons, and other decorations.
- Lace and heart designs for the borders and dividers.
- A cute and dreamy early-2000s inspired style.

## WEBSITE STRUCTURE

**Hello / Hero:** This is the introduction part of my website. It includes a welcome message, a short introduction, and my profile picture.

**About Me:** This section tells a little about myself, my personality, hobbies, and interests.

**My Path into Computing:** This part shares my experience and journey as a first-year BSCS student.

**Things I Love:** This section shows some of the things I like through pictures and decorative cards.

**Favorite Websites:** This includes links to some of the websites I like visiting, such as Spotify, ibisPaint, and Netflix.

**Favorites and Skills:** This section contains a list of my interests and skills, together with a table of my favorites.

**My Goals:** This part shows some of my personal, academic, and future goals.

**Reflection:** This is where I share what I learned while making the website and working with HTML and CSS.

**Footer:** The last part contains a closing message, my name, and links to go back to the top or the homepage.

## VISUAL AND INTERACTIVE FEATURES

- Made using HTML and CSS only.
- Pastel colors and gradient backgrounds.
- Decorative borders and dividers.
- Tangled-inspired pictures and designs.
- My profile picture in the introduction section.
- Hover effects on navigation links and other elements.
- Flexbox and Grid for arranging the content.
- Responsive design for smaller screens.
- Navigation links for moving between website sections.
- Image captions and alternative text for images.

## FILE ORGANIZATION

My project folder contains:

- `index.html` — Contains the main content and structure of my website.
- `style.css` — Contains the designs, colors, layout, and styling.
- `summary_Banday.txt` — Contains the summary and overview of my website.
- `images/` — Contains my profile picture and the other images used in the website.

## OVERALL PURPOSE

The purpose of my website is to introduce myself and share my interests, hobbies, favorite things, and goals. I also wanted to create a design that matches my personal style through a Tangled-inspired fairytale theme.

Through this project, I was able to practice using HTML and CSS, especially in creating webpage sections, styling elements, arranging content, adding navigation links, and making the website responsive.
