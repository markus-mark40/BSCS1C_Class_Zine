✧ a little introduction ✧

About Me

I'm someone who enjoys exploring different interests and trying out creative things. I can be quiet when I'm not around the right people, but I enjoy having random conversations and sharing thoughts with people I'm comfortable with. I also like spending time on my own, especially when I have something interesting to do.

Some of the things I enjoy are reading novels, doing digital art, playing online games, watching Western series, experimenting with makeup, and building with Legos. I also love listening to music, dressing up, and, of course, eating my favorite foods.

My interests can change depending on my mood, which is probably why I enjoy discovering new things. I like combining cute and dreamy aesthetics with a little mysterious or spooky touch.

✧ once upon a time ✧

My Journey into Computing

My journey into computing is still beginning. As a Computer Science student, I'm learning how computers work and how to create programs using code. There are times when programming feels challenging, especially when I encounter errors or concepts that I haven't understood yet.

However, I want to keep learning and improving. I know that becoming better at programming takes patience, practice, and a willingness to make mistakes. Every activity gives me another opportunity to understand something new.

I hope to become more confident in solving problems and creating projects of my own as I continue my journey in Computer Science.

Creating this webpage helped me understand the basics of HTML and CSS better.
