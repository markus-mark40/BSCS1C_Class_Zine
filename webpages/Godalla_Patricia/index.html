Reflection

I realized that programming is hard most especially the part where you don't just memorize each syntax, tags, or codes, you have to thoroughly understand it. You need to apply your analytical skills on what to add to the program, what to improve, and how to apply those codes on your own. You need to have creativity as well, to add some aesthetics and design on your page. And more importantly, you have to be PATIENT because it takes time to create and to build.

All throughout the process, I experienced a roller coaster of emotion and feelings. I got frustrated because I can't get the code right and the placement of it when I am adding the style. I got anxious thinking that I won't be able to finish it in a given period of time—fighting the urge to crash out. I felt happy because I am able to (somehow) REALLY understand how it works—those little joys keep me motivated in doing the tasks. I got confused, unmotivated, lazy, and other things that drove me insane. And honestly, all of those emotions that I felt are worth feeling (feeeeling~) due to the fact that it improved my page a LOT better than before.

My webpage improved big time in terms of the designs and the looks. It fits the decided theme (which is futuristic) but with a hint of my style. I actually had a hard time deciding what to add for the colors and the looks overall because I wanted it to be different from my past webpage. Also with the help of AI, I got to add some animations to my page because I was having a hard time understanding how it works and I am not fond of watching a video tutorial. Furthermore, I need to work on understanding CSS element sizing, as I often get confused about which measurement units and values to apply. Overall, I enjoyed and learned something while doing these tasks. Computer Science is getting more interesting.
