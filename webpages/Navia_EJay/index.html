my path.

When I was a kid, I loved playing computer games, but I never really thought about how computers worked. Throughout grade school and high school, I didn't develop much interest in computers either. My interests were more focused on traditional engineering fields, especially since most of my family members work in that industry. However, when it was time to choose a college course, I wasn't really sure what I wanted to pursue. I didn't have a dream course in mind, so I decided to explore something different from what most of my family had chosen. That's when I considered Computer Science. I saw it as a good choice because it offers a wide range of career opportunities and different paths to explore after graduation.

i want to learn.

I want to become more comfortable with programming and problem-solving through consistent practice until they become second nature to me. I also want to start building apps as early as possible in college so I can gain hands-on experience and have real projects even before I graduate. Aside from that, I want to learn more about web development and explore what I can create with it. I also want to learn cybersecurity like securing the networks and softwares.

  1. Data Structures

  2. Algorithms

  3. Databases

  4. APIs

  5. Git & Github

reflection.

While doing this activity, I realized that there are still a lot of technical terms that I'm not familiar with. I also realized how big web development is and how difficult it can be to master, but at the same time, it's amazing how you can bring your ideas to life. For this activity, I mostly focused on improving the design from my previous work and adding a lot of new things. I actually think I did a good job with the design because I was able to come up with my own ideas while also getting inspiration from other websites. I also realized that as long as you have ideas, you don't necessarily need to memorize every syntax to build a website.
